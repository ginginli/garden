How Garden Horizons Mutations Work

Understanding the mechanics behind Garden Horizons crop mutations.

Garden Horizons mutations are special modifiers that change the appearance and increase the selling value of your plants. Unlike growth stages (Unripe, Ripened, Lush) which every plant goes through naturally, Garden Horizons mutations are triggered by specific weather conditions and in-game events.

The best part? Garden Horizons plants are not limited to a single mutation — they can stack multiple Garden Horizons mutations at once. Garden Horizons mutation multipliers multiply together (not add), creating exponential growth in value. For example, Lush (3×) × Gold (5×) × Foggy (1.25×) = 18.75× the base price!

Weather changes every 5 minutes in Garden Horizons, synced with Bill's Shop restocks. During Garden Horizons weather events, plants in your garden have a chance to receive the corresponding Garden Horizons mutation. Each plant rolls its own independent chance for Garden Horizons mutations, so the number of plots you fill has no effect on per-plant odds.
