How Mutations Work

Understanding the mechanics behind crop mutations in Garden Horizons.

Mutations are special modifiers that change the appearance and increase the selling value of your plants. Unlike growth stages (Unripe, Ripened, Lush) which every plant goes through naturally, mutations are triggered by specific weather conditions and in-game events.

The best part? Plants are not limited to a single mutation — they can stack multiple mutations at once. Mutation multipliers add together (not multiply), then the total is multiplied by the variant level (growth stage + Silver/Gold traits).

Weather changes every 5 minutes in Garden Horizons, synced with Bill's Shop restocks. During weather events, plants in your garden have a chance to receive the corresponding mutation. Each plant rolls its own independent chance, so the number of plots you fill has no effect on per-plant odds.

Mutation Stacking Guide

Maximize your profits with these mutation stacking strategies.

📊

Sell Price Formula

Final Price = Base Sell × Variant Total × Mutation Total × (Weight ÷ Avg Weight)². Variant Total = Growth Stage + Silver (2.0×) + Gold (5.0×). Mutation Total = Sum of all mutation multipliers.

🌟

Additive Stacking

Mutations stack additively with each other. For example, Starstruck (6.5×) + Shocked (4.5×) = 11.0× mutation total. This is then multiplied by your variant total for massive profits!

💰

Lush + Gold Priority

Always wait for Lush growth stage — the 3.0× base variant multiplies all mutations. Gold + Lush = 8.0× variant total, making every mutation point worth 8× more. A Lush Gold plant with Starstruck gives 52× base price!

⚡

High-Tier Focus

High-tier mutations matter most. Starstruck (6.5×) alone with Lush Gold gives 8.0 × 6.5 = 52× the base price. Combine multiple high-tier mutations for even bigger multipliers!
